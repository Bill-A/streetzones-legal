StreetZones

Parking Enforcement Alerts System

StreetZones save drivers money and time by warning them about ticket writers, booting crews, and enforcement activity. This mobile app addresses the nationwide $96 billion parking crisis ($3B in tickets/fines, $20B+ in meter overpayments, and $73B in time wasted searching for parking).

Launching in January 2026 on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
